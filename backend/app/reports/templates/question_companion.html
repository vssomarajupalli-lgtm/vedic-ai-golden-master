Formula & Calculation Methods

transparency — existing methodology, no new calculations

How the values in this report are produced

  • Natal Promise — the domain-level foundation score computed by the existing NatalPromise engine from the natal D1 structure. Displayed as a percentage with a grade label using the single authoritative grade scale (EXCELLENT 80+ / VERY GOOD 65+ / GOOD 50+ / WEAK 35+ / TOO WEAK).
  • Planetary / House strength — existing planet and house strength factors, including the Ashtakavarga (BAV) adjustment layer where present (base score → BAV modifier → adjusted final score).
  • Current Support — the current Vimshottari period’s activation percentage computed by the existing Dasha engine from the weighting of the MD / AD / PD lord strengths (0.50 / 0.30 / 0.20).
  • Dasha contribution & the MD / AD / PD role — each row of the Planetary Periods timeline is a Vimshottari MD / AD / PD period; the Dasha activation of the period is one of the weighted factors of the master probability synthesis.
  • Activation / Probability — activation_pct is the period’s Dasha activation; final_probability_pct is the master probability synthesis score for that period (both from the existing engine outputs shown as the Future Opportunity Windows).
  • Grade interpretation — percentages and grades always travel together (e.g. “84% (Excellent)”); there is no second grading scale.
  • Special Transit / Mandali periods — Sade Sati, Ardha Ashtama Shani and Ashtama Shani status comes from the existing governed Mandali resolver, displayed verbatim (Elinati Shani is internal-only by project governance).
